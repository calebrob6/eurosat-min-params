## What I tried: spatial texture of per-pixel spectral-index maps
Every prior index feature was a per-band *mean* ratio — one scalar NDVI/NDWI/…
per patch — discarding all within-patch spatial structure. The new family
computes the **per-pixel index map** (NDVI, NDWI, NDBI, NDMI, NBR, BSI) and
summarises its heterogeneity across the 64×64 patch. Three sub-families were
tested on top of the submission-05 pool (`{split}_feat_o5.npy`, 281 features),
`experiments/idxtex_floor_sweep.py`, rule = smallest k with mean 10×5-fold
train-CV ≥ 0.940:

| family added | features | axis it captures | floor k | params | test |
|--------------|---------:|------------------|--------:|-------:|-----:|
| (base281, submission 05)          | —  | —                          | 38 | 390 | 0.9461 |
| **ixtex** (std, grad-mean, grad-std, spread of 6 index maps) | 24 | within-patch index heterogeneity | **34** | **350** | **0.9437** |
| ixtex2 (coarse std + grad-mean, 2×2-pooled)  | 12 | index heterogeneity @ coarse scale | 35 | 360 | 0.9456 |
| ixent (value-histogram entropy of 6 maps)    | 6  | index value spread          | 40 | 410 | 0.9441 |

- **ixtex is the winner**: it alone drops the floor 390 → 350 and boosts accuracy
  at *every* k (at k=38 it gives test 0.9502, the best test seen on any pool).
- **The crossing is sharp and seed-stable** (20-shuffle confirm,
  `experiments/confirm_ixtex20.py`): CV(k=33)=0.9394 ± 0.0001, CV(k=34)=0.9443 ±
  0.0001. k=34 clears 0.940 by ~40 SE — not seed luck. There is a genuine +0.005
  jump when the 34th feature (an index-gradient) enters the top-k.
- **The L1 selector pulls 9 of the 24 ixtex features into the top-34** — the
  gradient-magnitude and gradient-std of the NBR / NDWI / NDVI / BSI / NDMI maps
  dominate; no `std` (except NBR) and no `spread` feature is selected. So it is
  the *gradient* (edges) of the index maps that carries the signal, not the raw
  index std.
- **Combining with ixent / ixtex2 did NOT help** (same k=34 floor, mixed test),
  re-confirming the iteration-5 lesson: only pool a family if it helps *alone*;
  adding weak families dilutes the L1 top-k. ixent alone even HURTS (floor 410).

## Why index-map gradients are orthogonal (physical intuition)
NDVI(x,y) is the vegetation map of the patch. Its *mean* (already in the pool)
says "how green on average"; its *gradient* says "where do vegetation boundaries
run". A managed PermanentCrop is near-uniform (low index gradient); mixed
HerbaceousVegetation is patchy (high); a Highway is a sharp low-NDVI streak
across a green background (a strong localized index edge). Per-band gradients see
brightness edges; index gradients see *material-boundary* edges after the
band-ratio has cancelled illumination — a genuinely different edge map.

## Ranked ideas for next iterations (toward ~300 params / k=30)
1. **Directional index texture / index-map coherence & orientation.** ixtex used
   only isotropic gradient magnitude of the index maps. The same
   structure-tensor coherence and orientation-entropy that helped on raw bands
   (subs 03–04) applied to the NDVI/BSI *maps* could separate Highway's single
   dominant low-NDVI direction from isotropic field boundaries — directly
   targeting the Highway cap. Likely the strongest next lever.
2. **Prune the pool to de-noise the L1 top-k.** The pool is now 305 features but
   only 34 are deployed; many of the 65 percentile / redundant-gradient features
   are never selected and add L1-ranking noise. A curated smaller pool (drop
   percentiles the selector never picks) might qualify k=33 (340) or k=32 (330).
3. **Forward-selection / greedy-on-CV instead of L1-rank top-k.** L1 ranking is a
   proxy; a forward selector that directly maximises multi-seed CV could find a
   better 32–33-feature subset than L1's fixed order (watch for CV overfitting —
   use held-out shuffles).
4. **Index texture at a 2nd fine offset (lag-2 gradient of index maps).** ixtex
   uses only the offset-1 gradient; a coarser index gradient is orthogonal to it
   the way multi-scale band gradients stacked in submission 01.
5. **Still haven't used the 8 idle V100s.** For a >95% sub-500-param target a tiny
   distilled conv net remains the one unexplored direction; the linear frontier is
   now at 350 and each family buys less, so a fundamentally different model class
   may be needed to break well below 300.
