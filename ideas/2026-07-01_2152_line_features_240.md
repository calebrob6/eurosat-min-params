## What the family is and why it worked where 3 prior levers failed

`hough_line_features` (`src/features.py`): a Hough transform (== Radon of the
binary edge image) on the pan / NDVI / NDBI channels, summarised per channel by
`peakfrac` (longest collinear run / edge count), `peaklen` (longest line / patch
diagonal), `top3frac` (sum of best line at the 3 strongest orientations / edges).
9 features, zero learned parameters. Vectorised as a sparse projection matrix
`M @ edge_flat` (built once, reused for all patches); ~22s for the 16200-patch
train split.

**Why it is orthogonal.** Every directional feature already in the pool
(coherence, orientation entropy/histogram, index-map coherence/orient-entropy) is
*local* — it aggregates per-pixel gradient directions. Crop rows (many short
parallel edges) and a single long streak (Highway, River) are indistinguishable to
those: both have low orientation entropy / high coherence. The Hough peak is
*global collinearity* — the length of the single longest straight line — which no
local statistic sees. Per-class val means confirm the axis is real: River 0.178,
AnnualCrop 0.158 (long field boundaries), … Residential 0.088 (cluttered, no line);
the family fires most on the linear-structure classes.

**The decisive pre-screen.** Added to submission 09's frozen k=25 subset, a single
line feature lifts held-out **val 0.9404 → 0.9446** (+0.0042, >1σ_val) and
5×5-fold train-CV 0.9449 → 0.9466. This is exactly the held-out-val lift iters
9–12 proved was the binding constraint below 260 — and exactly what ixcoh (iter
11) failed to produce (ixcoh raised selCV but not val, and flipped sign with the
seed partition). The line signal survives on independent images, so it is not
selection noise.

**Only 1 of 9 line features survives to the floor:** `linet3_ndvi` (top-3 line
fraction on the NDVI edge map). Like every prior family win, a small number of
features from a genuinely new orthogonal axis moves the whole floor. The other 8
are redundant with each other / with existing texture and get dropped early
(ixcoh_in_set: 9→…→1 over the descent).

## Method notes / reusables

- `experiments/line_features_lib.py` — the family (also `linepan`, a 3-feature
  pan-only variant, unused). Promoted verbatim (bit-identical) into
  `src/features.py::hough_line_features` with a `hough_lines=True` flag on
  `patch_features` (appended after `index_texture`, so the pool order is
  o6[0:305] + line[305:314]).
- `experiments/backward_select.py` gained `POOL=o6+line`; the honest FORCE_IX
  two-partition descent is `backward_select_o6_line_p0force_result.txt` /
  `_p20force_result.txt`.
- The full 50→23 descent is ~50–90 min under shared-machine load (the per-row
  serial VERIFY+SELECT-CV in the parent is the bottleneck, ~2.5 min/step, not the
  parallel drop-search). Both partitions converging to the identical low-k subset
  is a strong robustness signal (the SELECT-CV surface is partition-stable here).
