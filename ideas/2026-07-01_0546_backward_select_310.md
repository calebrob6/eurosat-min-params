choose a feature) and the held-out val split.

- The selected 30 features **spread across every family** (index-map texture,

## Why backward-greedy beats L1-rank top-k
L1-rank fits ONE sparse model and freezes the coefficient-magnitude order; it
never asks "given these 33 kept, which 34th adds most?" Backward elimination
re-fits after every removal, so it accounts for redundancy: two features L1 ranks
high may be near-duplicates, and dropping one costs almost nothing — a saving
L1-rank cannot see. On EuroSAT this is worth ~4 features (40 params) at the 0.940
bar. It is a general, reusable selector (`src/select.py`), not EuroSAT-specific.

## Honesty caveat (important for the next iteration)
Backward-greedy is *guided by* CV, so the SELECT-CV is optimistically biased for
the chosen subset — never quote it as the floor. The honest floor must be read
off an estimate the greedy never optimised: the disjoint VERIFY seeds and/or the
held-out val. Both were required to clear 0.940 here. Pushing the greedy lower
(k<28) risks val slipping under 0.940; a forward+backward (stepwise) search or
a fresh orthogonal family would be needed to honestly break 290.

## Ranked ideas for next iterations (toward ~290 / k=28 and below)
1. **Stepwise (forward+backward) selection.** Backward-greedy is one-directional;
   allowing re-additions (plus-L-minus-R) could find a better k=28 subset whose
   val also clears 0.940, honestly breaking 290.
2. **Re-open the feature hunt now that selection is stronger.** Families that
   *looked* neutral under L1-rank top-k (e.g. `ixcoh`, which raised test but not
   the L1 floor) might lower the backward-greedy floor — re-sweep the candidate
   families through backward elimination instead of L1.
3. **C / regularisation per k.** All runs fixed C=10. Tuning C (honestly, on CV)
   at small k may buy another feature's worth of margin.
4. **The Highway/PermanentCrop cap is still the ceiling** (Highway ~0.86). A
   family that genuinely separates linear paved structures — not tried: Hough/line
   detection, or directional index texture at the *right* scale — remains the only
   route to a higher accuracy bar (95%+), which would also allow trimming more
   params at the current 94% bar.
5. **Still haven't used the 8 idle V100s.** A tiny distilled conv net is the one
   unexplored model class for a sub-300-param / >95% target.
