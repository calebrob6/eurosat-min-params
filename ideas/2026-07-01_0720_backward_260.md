## The low-k floor (both partitions, honest protocol)
Resuming from the verified k=28 subset and dropping the SELECT-CV-maximising
feature each step:

| k | params | verCV p0 / p20 | val p0 / p20 | test p0 / p20 | honest? |
|--:|-------:|----------------|--------------|---------------|---------|
| 28 | 290 | 0.9462 / 0.9462 | 0.9417 / 0.9415 | 0.9487 / 0.9478 | ✓ (sub 08) |
| 27 | 280 | 0.9459 / 0.9457 | **0.9420 / 0.9417** | 0.9472 / 0.9474 | ✓ widest val |
| 26 | 270 | 0.9455 / 0.9457 | 0.9407 / 0.9404 | 0.9474 / 0.9470 | ✓ |
| **25** | **260** | 0.9451 / 0.9450 | 0.9404 / 0.9407 | **0.9443 / 0.9459** | ✓ **floor** |
| 24 | 250 | 0.9440 / 0.9441 | 0.9396 / 0.9387 | 0.9452 / 0.9456 | ✗ val<0.940 |

Honest floor rule = smallest k with verCV≥0.940 AND val≥0.940. **k=25 clears on
both partitions; k=24 fails val on both.** verCV (+0.005) and test (+0.004) clear
with room at k=25; val is the binding check (+0.0004). Committed k=25 (260).

- **Non-monotone val is real and useful.** k=27 has *higher* val (0.9420/0.9417)
  than both k=28 AND k=26 — dropping one feature at the k=28→27 step (feat 94 on
  p0, 300 on p20) improves generalisation. So k=27 (280) is the *widest-margin*
  low-k option, beating sub 08's k=28 val while cutting 10 params; k=25 (260) is
  the most aggressive still-honest point. Submission 09 commits 260 and documents
  `--k 27` as the conservative fallback.

## Why floating (SFBS) is not worth more compute here
The partial floating traces (both partitions, killed at k≈36) show every backward
drop and floating re-addition landing selCV in 0.9487–0.9492 — indistinguishable
from pure backward at the same k. Floating's whole value is escaping the nesting
of pure backward, but on this pool the nested subsets are already ~optimal for the
CV metric, so re-additions only shuffle within noise. Combined with its 2–3× cost,
**SFBS is a documented dead end for lowering the o6 floor.** (It remains a correct,
reusable selector in `src/select.py` for pools where nesting bites harder.)

## Ranked ideas for iteration 10 (toward ~250 / k=24 and below)
1. **The o6 pool + backward-greedy is now genuinely at its floor (k=25).** k=24
   fails val on both partitions by ~0.0005–0.0013. Going lower needs *either* a new
   parameter-free feature that lifts the whole low-k curve, *or* a different head.
   The feature lever looked exhausted at k=34 (iter 7) but that was under L1-rank;
   re-running the candidate-family floor sweep through **backward-greedy at k=24–26**
   (not L1-rank) is the untested combination — `ixcoh` raised test at every k yet
   never moved the L1 floor, a hint it may move the backward floor.
2. **C / regularisation per k (still untried).** All runs fix C=10. At k=25 the
   model may be mis-regularised; a cheap honest C-sweep on VERIFY-CV could widen the
   razor-thin val margin at k=25 or legitimise k=24. Lowest-effort next step.
3. **Highway / PermanentCrop remain the accuracy ceiling** (~0.88 / ~0.90). A
   line/Hough family targeting paved linear structures is the only route to a 95%+
   bar, which would in turn free params at the 94% bar.
4. **The 8 idle V100s are still unused.** A tiny distilled conv net is the one
   unexplored model class for a sub-250-param / >95% target.
